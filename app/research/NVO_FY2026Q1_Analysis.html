Richard Vosser（JPMorgan）
口服Wegovy 4mg→9mg滴定率下滑跡象與海外供應能否支撐
▼
問題核心
口服Wegovy上市強勁，但病患從4mg調升到9mg之間似乎出現滴定率下滑——是價格跳升、耐受性還是減重成效導致？這會如何影響用藥留存時間與後續成長？另外海外市場已宣布上市，供應能撐多少個市場、供應狀況如何？
管理層回應
Jamey Millar：整體滴定表現符合預期，與注射型Wegovy相當，也持續看到向9mg、25mg劑量的週度成長。Karsten Munk Knudsen：口服Wegovy因產品設計本身供應並非無限，但即便如此仍能在今年宣布多個海外首波市場上市，反映供應規模化與庫存布局的成果，後續國際擴張步調將是漸進式。
⚑ 邏輯缺口
Richard明確指出「4mg到9mg之間的滴定率下滑（drop-off）」這個具體現象，Jamey的回應卻只是泛稱「滴定表現符合預期、與注射劑相當」，完全沒有正面回應分析師觀察到的這個特定下滑模式，等於用一句通用的安心話術帶過具體問題。另外Karsten罕見親口承認「我們並沒有無限供應」——這與公司對外一貫強調的爆發式上量敘事存在張力，但管理層並未量化供應瓶頸的實際嚴重程度或何時緩解。
逐字稿原文
Richard Vosser (JPMorgan): "You've seen very strong uptake, there seems to be a bit of a drop-off in the patients going between the 4 mg and the 9 mg doses in terms of titration. Just wanted your views on what might be behind that... How many markets do you think you can sustain and generally, how's supply going?"

Jamey Millar: "Overall, titration is happening as expected and comparable to what we see with injectable Wegovy, so we're pleased with the progress there... We also see movement toward the 9 mg and 25 mg doses, continued uptake on a week-over-week basis."

Karsten Munk Knudsen: "While we do not have unlimited supply for the Wegovy pill due to the product design... we're still able to announce launches in the first markets ex-U.S. already this year... Then we play it gradually from here in terms of the pace of international rollout."
Sachin Jain（Bank of America）
SG&A創數季新低的合理性與口服定價「甜蜜點」是否僅限起始劑量
▼
問題核心
SG&A是近幾季最低的絕對金額，在產品上市期間顯得不尋常——是新的電銷/遠距醫療商業模式帶來的常態基準，還是全年費用節奏問題？另外「無意降價、口服定價已在甜蜜點」的說法，是否僅限於149/150元起始低劑量？若口服是甜蜜點，價格組合怎麼往這個低價區間移動、對注射劑與糖尿病定價有何啟示？
管理層回應
Karsten：本季有一筆逾DKK1億的法律撥備調整一次性挹注SG&A；口服Wegovy上市資源已「幾乎全力投入」（含Super Bowl廣告），遠距醫療夥伴模式使自有媒體與付費媒體的規模效益不同，本季聲量（share of voice）極高，全年SG&A比率預期落在20出頭%。Mike Doustdar：目前定價已是「甜蜜點」——16週2百萬張處方、單週逾20萬張，「這個定價完全正確」；但長期隨滲透擴大到數億病患規模，價格勢必需要下調。
⚑ 邏輯缺口
Sachin具體追問「無意降價」是否僅針對149/150美元的低劑量起始價，Mike完全沒有正面回答這個子問題，只重申整體定價哲學的方向性敘述，迴避了分析師真正想釐清的「哪個劑量帶」適用範圍。同時，管理層一方面強調SG&A已「幾乎全力投入」上市資源，另一方面本季SG&A卻是數季最低的絕對金額——這個組合只靠「遠距醫療模式規模效益不同」帶過，沒有給出付費媒體vs自有媒體的具體佔比或金額佐證，是敘事層面的自我調和而非數字驗證。
逐字稿原文
Sachin Jain (Bank of America): "It's the lowest absolute number for a number of quarters. Feels a little bit odd into a product launch... Is the no intention to lower a comment for 150, 149 lower oral dose holding? If oral price is a sweet spot, how do we think about price mix across the business shifting to this lower price bracket?"

Karsten Munk Knudsen: "We have a one-off, which is favorable in the fact that we are adjusting a legal provision, which has a benefit of... a notch more than DKK 100 million... we have gone pretty much all in in terms of the Wegovy pill launch and resourcing of that. You've seen the Super Bowl ads... In terms of SG&A ratio, we're in the low 20s for the full year."

Mike Doustdar: "We are seeing a situation where at the current prices, we have had 2 million scripts after 16 weeks, more than 200,000 scripts per week... At this point, I would say we have priced this product perfectly correct... if your ambition is to get to hundreds of millions of patients at one point or the other, at that point, of course, the prices will be very different."

管理層回應
Jamey：價格彈性如Mike所述已在甜蜜點，帶動創紀錄的處方量；病患決策關鍵仍是減重幅度（口服Wegovy達17%），加上起始劑量的限時優惠定價也吸引病患。Karsten：本季R&D比率偏低，全年將持續加碼投資。

Simon Baker (Rothschild & Co): "In light of the launch and success of the launch, if you're getting a better handle on this price volume dynamic... is it convenience? Is it efficacy? Or is it cost because it's the cheapest option in the market at the moment?"

Jamey Millar: "The key decision criteria still is magnitude of weight loss, and the Wegovy pill demonstrated 17% weight loss. In addition, the attractiveness of the limited time offer pricing for the initial starting dose brings people to the product as well."

Karsten Munk Knudsen: "The R&D ratio in the quarter is a notch on the low side compared to what we expect for the full year. Expect us to lean in on R&D investment in the coming quarters."

▼
問題核心
Ozempic Q1表現強勁，美國價格侵蝕狀況如何？一旦Medicare GLP-1 Bridge計畫上路，糖尿病用Ozempic下半年是否會受到外溢衝擊？另外財測下緣為何維持不變——Q1表現穩健，是什麼變數讓公司不願上修雙位數衰退的下緣？
管理層回應
Karsten：Ozempic美國價格侵蝕約-10%至-15%，與去年底、全年展望的說法一致，沒有變化。財測方面，三個月過去後看到多項利多逐步兌現（口服處方趨勢、Wegovy HD美國核准、決定在海外選定市場推出口服Wegovy、競爭與專利到期資訊更明朗），因此上下限同步上移1個百分點，應視為信心訊號；坦承區間仍偏寬。
⚑ 邏輯缺口
分析師實際問的是「Medicare Bridge上路後是否會拖累糖尿病用Ozempic下半年銷售」，Karsten的回答完全繞過這個前瞻性問題，只重複過去到現在-10%至-15%價格侵蝕的歷史趨勢描述，沒有對Bridge計畫可能造成的糖尿病適應症外溢風險給出任何前瞻評估。財測部分，Karsten自己承認「區間確實偏寬」，卻只用「這代表信心」帶過，並未解釋究竟什麼具體下行情境仍支撐著-12%這個下緣沒有跟著上修——真正的尾部風險被留白。
逐字稿原文
Michael Leuchten (Jefferies): "Can you talk to the price erosion in the U.S. you saw in Q1, and how should that or would that look like once the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge kicks in? Would there be collateral damage for Ozempic in diabetes in the second half?... you haven't changed the lower end of the range, when Q1 came in quite robustly. What's the variables that doesn't allow you to lift that lower-end double-digit decline?"

Karsten Munk Knudsen: "We're looking at these, call it -10% to -15% price erosion. Really no change there... we parallel shifted both the sales and OP ranges by one percentage point. You should see that as increased confidence. Yes, I'm with you that the range is a notch wide, but you should take it as a signal of confidence that we lifted the range by one percentage point."

Ziltivekimab四項試驗機會排序與Wegovy專利防禦布局的落差
▼
問題核心
ZEUS、Hermes、Artemis、Athena四項ziltivekimab試驗中，哪一項機會最大？另外Wegovy美國IP方面，公司一貫以semaglutide化合物專利到期日作為LOE基準，但裝置/劑型專利的到期日比它晚了近十年——公司打算捍衛這些較晚到期的專利嗎？
管理層回應
Martin：僅ZEUS、Hermes、Artemis三項會帶來潛在適應症（分別為ASCVD、HFpEF、MI後），對生物學機轉信心高，但強調這是首創機轉（first-in-class抗IL-6）、風險同樣高，安全性與耐受性仍待驗證。Karsten：產業慣例是早期高風險研發投入換取專利保護期，一旦取得專利便會在法院捍衛，若遭學名藥挑戰會依法捍衛全部專利。
⚑ 邏輯缺口
分析師明確問了四項試驗（含Athena），Martin的回答卻悄悄只列出三項（ZEUS/Hermes/Artemis），Athena被直接略過而未作任何解釋，讀者無法得知Athena的定位或為何被排除在「會產生適應症」的名單外。另外Karsten對IP問題的回應是產業教科書式的通用說法（「專利核准後會依法捍衛」），完全沒有正面處理分析師提出的具體量化落差——化合物專利與裝置/劑型專利之間近十年的到期日差距，究竟公司是否相信後者能獨立阻擋學名藥進入，是這題的核心，卻被繞開。
逐字稿原文
Mike Nedelcovych (TD Cowen): "Between ZEUS, Hermes, Artemis, and Athena, which trial you view as having the largest opportunity... there's almost a decade difference between the drug substance patent and the latest dated patents for Wegovy pill and Wegovy injection. My question is, does Novo plan to defend that IP?"

Martin Holst Lange: "ZEUS, Hermes, and Artemis are the only ones that will lead to potential indications, respectively in ASCVD, heart failure with preserved ejection fraction and post-myocardial infarction... We still have to flag the high risk. This is first in class."

Karsten Munk Knudsen: "When granted, of course, we intend to defend all our patents in court, should they be challenged by generics."

James Gordon (Barclays): "In India, I think it's at least eight generics approved and some very low prices, at least for the vial form, just DKK 14... do you still think like long term with synthesis generics would struggle to match you on price...The second question was just... you did a 47% adjusted EBIT margin... the midpoint of the full year guide is more like 40%. Is it because of lots more R&D, or is it also like you're allowing for doing a load more sales and marketing?"

Karsten Munk Knudsen: "We believe that we are hypercompetitive in terms of unit cost in producing sema... I think India is not a good proxy for other markets, to be honest... Being at, call it 40% operating margin or 40% plus is already very high. Strategically for us as a company, it's more important for us to invest in future growth more so than short-term margin optimization."

James Quigley（Goldman Sachs）
口服Wegovy早期市場回饋與AMAZE試驗劑量彈性設計是否真正吸取CagriSema教訓
▼
問題核心
口服Wegovy上市初期，醫師與病患對食物/飲水交互作用管理的最新回饋如何？兩款產品之間減重差異的認知程度如何？另外AMAZE已在clinicaltrials.gov啟動七項第三期試驗，肥胖試驗的給藥方案設計吸取了CagriSema計畫（給藥不可預測性）的哪些教訓、劑量彈性如何？而且這是在REDEFINE 11數據讀出之前就要回答的問題。
管理層回應

長期需求場景的因果鏈
